Privacy Policy

robjects — last updated August 16, 2026

This policy describes how robjects handles information from customers who contact us or sign up for our services. The BB Notifier app itself stores no visitor information.

What information we collect

We collect contact information that you provide to us, such as your name, phone number, and email address.

How we collect it

We collect this information through forms on our website and app, and when you contact us directly (for example, by email or text message).

Why we collect it and how we use it

We use this information to provide and operate our services, to respond to your inquiries, and to send service-related messages that you request, such as notifications, scheduling, and confirmations.

Sharing

We do not sell customer information. At robjects, customer information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with the service providers that help us operate — our messaging provider, our email provider, and our hosting provider — and only to the extent they need it to do that work. We may also disclose information where required by law.

At robjects, all sharing mentioned in this policy excludes mobile opt-in and consent; opt-in information is never shared with anyone for any purpose.

Opting out

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, or QUIT to any message from us (reply HELP for help). You can opt out of emails by using the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

Questions and requests

To ask about our privacy practices, or to request access to, changes to, or deletion of your information, contact us at robjects@protonmail.com.
